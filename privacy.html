Touch Hero 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日

Touch Hero 尊重并保护您的隐私。本应用不要求注册或登录，不包含广告、用户跟踪或分析 SDK，也不会把您的个人数据或素材上传到开发者服务器。

1. 数据收集

本应用不收集用户数据，不进行跨 App 或网站跟踪，也不会出售或共享用户数据。

2. 本机存储

设备绑定信息、快捷场景、显示设置、本地素材索引和预览缓存可能保存在您的 iPhone 上，仅用于提供应用功能。这些内容不会发送给开发者。您可以在应用内删除相关素材，或通过卸载应用移除本机数据。

3. 照片、视频与相机

只有在您主动选择或拍摄素材时，本应用才会请求访问照片、相机或麦克风。图片和视频在 iPhone 本机完成裁切和格式转换；视频生成尾标素材时会移除声音。原始文件不会上传到云端。

4. 蓝牙与本地网络

本应用使用蓝牙连接兼容的 Touch Hero 尾标设备，并通过设备提供的临时 Wi-Fi 和本地网络地址直接传输素材、预览和固件。该通信仅发生在您的 iPhone 与您连接的硬件之间，不经过开发者服务器。设备临时网络可能不提供互联网访问。

5. CarPlay

CarPlay 功能仅用于显示控制选项和设备状态，并向已连接的尾标设备发送用户选择的控制指令。本应用不会通过 CarPlay 收集个人数据。

6. 技术支持邮件

如果您主动发送技术支持邮件，我们只会使用您提供的邮箱地址和邮件内容来回复并处理该支持请求。邮件由您和所使用的电子邮件服务提供商传输，不属于本应用自动收集的数据。

7. 政策更新

如果应用功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面及其生效日期。

8. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请联系：2801541819@qq.com

Touch Hero Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Touch Hero respects your privacy. The app does not require an account or sign-in, contains no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs, and does not upload your personal data or media to developer-operated servers.

1. Data Collection

The app does not collect user data, track users across apps or websites, or sell or share user data.

2. On-Device Storage

Device bindings, quick scenes, display settings, local media indexes, and preview caches may be stored on your iPhone solely to provide app functionality. This information is not sent to the developer. You can remove related media within the app or remove local app data by uninstalling the app.

3. Photos, Videos, and Camera

The app requests access to photos, the camera, or the microphone only when you choose or capture media. Images and videos are cropped and converted on your iPhone. Audio is removed when video material is generated for the display. Original files are not uploaded to the cloud.

4. Bluetooth and Local Network

The app uses Bluetooth to connect to compatible Touch Hero display hardware and uses the device's temporary Wi-Fi network to transfer media, previews, and firmware directly over the local network. Communication occurs only between your iPhone and the hardware you connect, without passing through developer-operated servers. The temporary device network may not provide Internet access.

5. CarPlay

CarPlay is used only to present controls and device status and to send commands you select to the connected display. The app does not collect personal data through CarPlay.

6. Support Email

If you voluntarily contact support, we use the email address and message content you provide only to respond to and resolve your request. Email is transmitted by you and your email provider and is not data automatically collected by the app.

7. Policy Changes

If app functionality or data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact 2801541819@qq.com.
